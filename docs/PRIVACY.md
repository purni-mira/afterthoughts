# Privacy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Afterthoughts is built to keep your thinking yours.

**What's stored, and where.** Your afterthoughts, voice profile, name and headline, and settings are stored only in your browser on your device (localStorage). There is no Afterthoughts account and no Afterthoughts database. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so export a backup from Settings if you want to keep them.

**What leaves your device.** Only when you use an AI provider:

- **Demo mode:** nothing leaves your device.
- **Your own key (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI):** the text of each request (the source you pasted, your answers, your take, and your writing samples when drafting) is sent directly from your browser to that provider, under their terms and privacy policy. Your key is stored in your browser and sent only to that provider.
- **Site owner's server:** the same request text goes to the server of whoever hosts the app, which forwards it to their AI provider. The bundled server code doesn't store requests.
- **Link fetch** (server mode only): the link you enter is fetched by the server to read the article.

**What we never do.** No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no selling of data. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

**Voice input.** If you use the microphone button, your browser's speech recognition service turns speech into text. In Chrome, that audio is processed by Google.

**Your controls.** Export, import or delete everything in Settings → Your data.
